## Brand & Style
This design system defines an elite, future-forward visual environment tailored for high-performance software engineering, artificial intelligence products, and next-generation independent game design. It communicates technical mastery, razor-sharp computational precision, and bespoke digital luxury. 

The aesthetic synthesizes **Cyber Minimalism** with **Luxurious Glassmorphism**. Rather than falling into juvenile cyberpunk clichés or chaotic neon saturations, it uses pitch-dark obsidian and midnight canvases overlaid with crystalline blurred surfaces, delicate linear grid frameworks, and controlled prismatic luminescence. The emotional tone evokes the quiet confidence of an advanced skunkworks engineering laboratory—calculated, visionary, and hyper-polished.

## Colors
The color hierarchy is anchored by deep void blues and oceanic midnights (`#080D20` and `#0B1530`), forming an abyssal background that eliminates visual noise and heightens perceived depth. 

Chromatic power is channeled deliberately through twin energy axes:
- **Kinetic Intelligence (Cyan to Electric Blue):** `#12D8F5` transitioning to `#258BFF`, representing algorithmic throughput, execution, and active states.
- **Creative Synthesis (Purple to Violet):** `#873BDA` shifting into `#A855F7`, signifying world-building, engine computation, and game logic.

Text hierarchy is strictly maintained: `#F5F7FF` ensures maximum legibility for high-contrast scanning, while muted silver-blue `#A5B1CC` handles structural framing, meta labels, and secondary guidance. Glowing accents must be deployed with restraint, serving as edge lighting, focused beacons, or micro-interaction triggers rather than wide background flooding.

## Typography
The typography strategy enforces a tri-font structure balanced for high-tech product credibility:
- **Headlines (Sora):** Geometrically constructed, wide letterforms that project futurism, architectural balance, and structural authority. Used for hero titles, section landmarks, and impact figures.
- **Body & Continuous Reading (Manrope):** A clean, modern sans-serif with natural humanist proportions that prevents reading fatigue across documentation, product copy, and analytics dashboards.
- **Technical Accents & Metadata (JetBrains Mono):** Injected for parameters, system logs, code snippets, micro-tags, numerical counters, and studio mantras. Always uppercase or mono-aligned to emphasize precision engineering.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout model employs a strict 12-column responsive fluid grid structured with mathematical rhythm:
- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12 columns, 64px outer margins, 24px gutters. Elements align to dynamic modular tracks designed to showcase complex interfaces and cinematic game assets side by side.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8 columns, 32px outer margins, 20px gutters. Multi-column cards collapse to dual columns.
- **Mobile (320px - 767px):** 4 columns, 20px outer margins, 16px gutters. Structural hierarchy shifts to vertical sequential stacks with sticky primary actions.

Vertical layout sections rely on expansive spacing tiers (`4rem` to `8rem`) between modules, providing breathing room that keeps deep dark-mode compositions from feeling congested or dense.

## Elevation & Depth
Elevation abandons traditional drop-shadowing in favor of deep volumetric glassmorphism, multi-planar stacking, and perimeter refraction:

1. **Surface 0 (Substrate Canvas):** `#080D20` solid canvas punctuated by hairline SVG gridlines (`rgba(37, 139, 255, 0.04)` spaced at 48px intervals).
2. **Surface 1 (Base Container):** `#0B1530` with 0.70 alpha opacity, backdrop-filter blur of `16px`, and an ultra-subtle border stroke of `1px solid rgba(165, 177, 204, 0.12)`.
3. **Surface 2 (Interactive Floating Panel):** Translucent composite gradient (`rgba(11, 21, 48, 0.85)` to `rgba(20, 32, 68, 0.65)`), backdrop-filter blur of `24px`, topped with a directional light border (`1px solid rgba(37, 139, 255, 0.35)` along top and left edges, tapering to `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)` on bottom and right).
4. **Surface 3 (Overlays & Modals):** Backdrop-filter blur of `32px`, bordered by dual-phase neon accent lines with a diffused ambient glow (`box-shadow: 0 16px 48px -8px rgba(8, 13, 32, 0.8), 0 0 24px -4px rgba(37, 139, 255, 0.2)`).

## Shapes
The shape grammar adopts precise, rounded geometry (`0.5rem` / `8px` baseline) that harmonizes mechanical precision with ergonomic softness. 

- **Primary Cards & Panels:** Apply `rounded-lg` (`1rem` / `16px`) to balance large structural bounding boxes without clipping internal UI telemetry.
- **Badges, Tags, and Pill Buttons:** Utilize complete radial rounding (`rounded-full`) for high-contrast contrast against rectangular layout grids.
- **Data Tables & Code Windows:** Anchor to crisp `rounded-sm` (`0.25rem` / `4px`) corners to reflect rigid computational data structures.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Cyber Action:** Background gradient transitioning 135deg from `#258BFF` to `#873BDA`. Text styled in `#F5F7FF` (`button-text`). Border: `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.25)`. Hover triggers an ambient luminous aura (`box-shadow: 0 0 20px rgba(37, 139, 255, 0.45)`) and a subtle 1px upward translateY shift.
- **Secondary Ghost Action:** Semi-transparent fill (`rgba(11, 21, 48, 0.6)`), border: `1px solid rgba(37, 139, 255, 0.3)`. Text in `#F5F7FF`. Hover initiates border transition to `#12D8F5` and text tint shift.
- **Glass Utility Button:** Borderless `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)` backdrop with `backdrop-filter: blur(8px)`. Used strictly for icon-only toggles and system tray options.

### Chips & Telemetry Badges
Constructed with `#0B1530` at 80% opacity, bordered by `1px solid rgba(18, 216, 245, 0.3)`. Typography strictly uses `label-badge` in JetBrains Mono. Prefixed with a pulsating 6px circular LED indicator that cycles between Cyan (active systems) and Violet (automation pipelines).

### Input Fields
Darkened inset troughs (`#060A18`) with a recessed border (`1px solid rgba(165, 177, 204, 0.2)`). Focus state dissolves the border into an electric cyan highlight (`#12D8F5`) accompanied by an inner glow of `0 0 12px rgba(18, 216, 245, 0.15)`. Placeholder copy styled in `#A5B1CC` at 50% opacity.

### Interactive Glass Cards
Features dual-gradient background layering with subtle top-to-bottom falloff. Hovering initiates a directional perimeter highlight via CSS pseudo-element border transitions from Electric Blue to Violet. Interior content maintains generous breathing room (`space-lg`), separating headline metrics from descriptive copy with hairline divider rules (`1px solid rgba(165, 177, 204, 0.08)`).

### Selection Controls (Checkboxes & Radios)
Squircle and circular forms surfaced in `#0B1530` with `1px solid rgba(37, 139, 255, 0.4)`. Checked states snap into a solid `#258BFF` core with a centered crisp white glyph and an exterior diffuse pulse.

### Code & Terminal Inspector
A signature component dedicated to engineering workflows and game scripting previews. Features a darkened acrylic top toolbar hosting triple status beacons (slate, cyan, purple), a tabbed navigation bar rendered in `label-code`, and syntax-highlighted code bodies nestled over a sub-black (`#040711`) background.
